Pyramid

Object

Move all cards to the single foundation.

Rules

The object is to use up all the cards by placing them on the single foundation.

The cards are dealt face-up in a pyramid of seven rows (one card in the top row, seven in the bottom row), each card overlapping two cards of the row below. A card can only be played when no other card covers it. The remaining cards are the stock: click it to turn one card at a time onto the waste pile, whose top card can be paired with a free card in the pyramid. Two redeals are allowed.

You can only put a card on top of another card when the numerical values of the two cards adds up to 13. Queen is worth 12 and Jack is worth 11. You do not have to follow suit.

All Kings go off alone.

Notes

Quickplay is disabled for this game.
