Settings and Options

PySol contains a lot of options allowing you to customize your game's look and feel, and experience. These are found in the Options menu of your menu bar. Note that depending on your installation configuration, some of these options may not be available.

Play Options

Choose Options / Play options to open a window with the play options as check boxes. Change as many as you like, then press OK. Options that do not apply to the current game are grayed out.

Automatic play lets certain moves play themselves: auto face-up flips exposed cards face-up, auto deal deals cards from the talon when the waste is empty, and auto drop moves cards to the foundations when possible (even when it is off, auto drop can still be triggered by hand). Quick play enables the right mouse button shortcut. For more detail on auto drop, see Assist Options.

Assist has Shade legal moves, which highlights the stacks that can accept the cards you are moving. When no moves are left, the game tells you so.

Click to move cards moves cards by clicking a card and then clicking where it should go, instead of dragging. Confirm before quitting makes the app ask before it throws away a game you have not finished, for example when you start a new game or quit.

The last group holds options for specific games, such as dealing all cards in Accordion type games or showing removed tiles in Mahjongg games. More info on these can be found with the rules of said games.

Sound

The Sound item in the Options menu turns the game's sound effects on and off. To change how loud they are, use your device's volume control.

Cards and Table

Decks, Backs, and Background Color

Choose Options / Layout / Cards / Decks to change the look and feel of the individual cards. Different decks (cardsets) are available for the different kinds of games, and the Backs item next to it lets you pick a different design for the back of the cards.

You can also create custom cardsets from your own images - see cardset customization for more info on how.

The background color controls the color behind the cards. Choose Options / Layout / Background color and pick any color.

Card Size

The cards are scaled automatically to fit the window, so resize the window to make them larger or smaller.

Shadows

Moving cards always show a shadow under them.

Layout

The Options / Layout menu has these items:

  • Toolbar - the toolbar position, icon size, labels and visible buttons
  • Cards - the decks and card backs
  • Background color - picks the color behind the cards
  • Spread stacks - spreads the stacks evenly across the window

The window size and position are remembered separately for each game.

Animations

From here, you can set the animation speed (fast, medium or slow) or turn animations off entirely.

Fonts and Colors

The fonts follow your system fonts, and the text on the table switches between white and black to suit the background color.

Toolbar and Status Bar

The View menu has Show toolbar, Show status bar and Full screen (F11). On a Mac, macOS adds its own Enter Full Screen item to the View menu (Ctrl-Cmd-F).

Options / Layout / Toolbar configures the toolbar: which side of the window it is on, the size of the icons, whether the icons have labels, and which buttons are visible.

Where Settings are Stored

PySol settings are stored in a configuration file, options.dat or options.cfg depending on your environment - this file is located in the user folder.

You may back up this file, but editing it manually is not recommended and can break your configuration.
