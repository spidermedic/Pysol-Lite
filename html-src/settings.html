Settings and Options

PySol contains a lot of options allowing you to customize your game's look and feel, and experience. These are found in the Options menu of your menu bar. Note that depending on your installation configuration, some of these options may not be available.

Player Options

You can change your player name. By registering multiple players, you can track statistics for multiple players playing on the same computer separately.

Additionally, there are options for whether we're tracking statistics, or if we want to confirm before ending an unfinished game.

Automatic Play

Choose Options / Automatic play to open a window with the options as check boxes; options that do not apply to the current game are grayed out.

Automatic play options allow you to have certain moves play automatically, such as auto flip causing exposed cards to automatically flip face-up, and auto deal, which automatically deals cards from the talon when the waste is empty. Quick play enables the right mouse button shortcut.

The auto drop option allows cards to automatically move to the foundations when possible, though even if it's not set to run automatically, auto drop can still be manually triggered when needed. For more detail on auto drop, see Assist Options.

See How to use PySol for more details.

Assist Level

Choose Options / Assist level to open a window with all the assist options as check boxes. Change as many as you like, then press OK. The options allow you to enable or disable certain assist features, which causes their options to be grayed out, and keyboard shortcuts to not work.

The free hints option ensures that using hints does not disqualify the game from being counted as a win, though it would still not be counted as a perfect game.

You can also enable a stuck notification, so the game will notify you if there are no moves left.

There are other assist options that are game-specific, such as dealing all cards in Accordion type games or showing removed tiles in Mahjongg games. More info on these can be found with the rules of said games.

See Assist Options for more details.

Sound

The Sound item in the Options menu turns the game's sound effects on and off. To change how loud they are, use your device's volume control.

Cards and Table

Decks, Backs, and Background Color

Choose Options / Cards / Decks to change the look and feel of the individual cards. Different decks (cardsets) are available for the different kinds of games, and the Backs item next to it lets you pick a different design for the back of the cards.

You can also create custom cardsets from your own images - see cardset customization for more info on how.

The background color controls the color behind the cards. Choose Options / Layout / Background color and pick any color.

Card Size

The cards are scaled automatically to fit the window, so resize the window to make them larger or smaller.

Shade Legal Moves

The Shade legal moves option in the Assist level window highlights the destination stacks that can accept the cards you are moving. Moving cards always show a shadow under them.

Layout

The Options / Layout menu has two items:

  • Background color - picks the color behind the cards
  • Spread stacks - spreads the stacks evenly across the window

The window size and position are remembered separately for each game.

Animations

From here, you can set the animation speed (fast, medium or slow) or turn animations off entirely.

Mouse

By default you move cards by dragging them. Choose Options / Click to move to move cards by clicking a card and then clicking where it should go, instead of dragging.

Fonts, Colors, and Timeouts

Here, you can alter the fonts and text styles used throughout the app, or the colors in the game. By changing timeouts, you can speed up or slow down the delay between certain automatic actions.

Toolbar and Status Bar

You can configure the toolbar on the top of the window. From here, you can turn on and off the toolbar or move it to a different side of the screen.

Additionally, you can decide which icons you want to have on the toolbar. You can control the toolbar configuration - the size of the icons, or if the icons are displayed with labels.

Similarly, you can turn on or off the status bar at the bottom of the window.

Where Settings are Stored

PySol settings are stored in a configuration file, options.dat or options.cfg depending on your environment - this file is located in the user folder. See Installation for more details on the exact pathing.

You may back up this file, but editing it manually is not recommended and can break your configuration.
